## EchoVela 0.3.1

Smaller steps at the quiet end of the keys.

- The brightness and volume keys now move 5% at a time (100, 95, 90 and so on down to 5), then 1% at a time below that (4, 3, 2, 1, 0). Volume no longer jumps from 6% straight to off.
- Option and Shift together still move 1% at a time.

Your settings and presets stay as they are.
