## EchoVela 0.3.3

The volume slider is easier to trust.

- **One volume slider that follows the sound.** The menu-bar panel says where your Mac's sound is playing. On a monitor's speakers, the slider changes the monitor's own volume. On your MacBook's speakers, headphones or AirPods, it changes your Mac's volume.
- **The volume slider no longer disappears after a few hours.** When your displays change or your Mac wakes, EchoVela asks your monitor about its settings again. A monitor that's slow to answer used to lose its volume (and sometimes its contrast) until you reopened EchoVela. Now EchoVela keeps the last settings and asks again a few times.
- **Volume changes reach the monitor.** EchoVela checks that the monitor took each volume change. If it didn't, EchoVela sends it again or reconnects. If the monitor still doesn't answer, the panel says so, with a **Check Again** button.
- Opening the panel asks a monitor again about anything missing.

Your settings and presets stay as they are.
