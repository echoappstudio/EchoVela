## EchoVela 0.3.0

Lumen is now **EchoVela**, with a new look.

- A new icon: a silver sun on black.
- Black, charcoal and silver throughout, light or dark to match your Mac.
- A new menu bar picture and a redesigned panel: one card for each display, silver sliders and a solid silver chip for the preset in use.
- The window has a sidebar for Displays, Presets, Shortcuts and Settings.
- The brightness bar in the corner of the screen is silver.

This is a fresh install, not an update to the old Lumen. Download `EchoVela.zip`, drag EchoVela into Applications, and allow it in Accessibility again to use the brightness keys. Your old settings and presets are not carried over; delete the old copy first.
